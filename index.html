    How it works

    From a chat to a live site.

    1. Message me on WhatsApp. Tell me about your business and what you want customers to do: book, order, or just find you.
    2. I build it with your details. Your name, photos, prices and colours. You see it on your phone before anything goes live.
    3. We adjust it together. Change the words, swap photos, move things around until it feels like you.
    4. It goes live. Share the link everywhere. If you have an owner page, I show you how to run it yourself.
